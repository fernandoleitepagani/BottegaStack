# Minimize reader load
Short sentences, plain words, one idea per line.

- Lead with the answer or action, then the reason.
- Cut jargon, filler and examples that restate the rule.
- Use headings and lists so the reader can scan.
- Say the same thing once, in the place the reader needs it.
